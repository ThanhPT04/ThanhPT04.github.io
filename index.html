About Me

Passionate MC · Storyteller · Bridge Builder

Xin chào! I'm Phương Thúy Thanh.

A bilingual MC with a deep passion for connecting people through storytelling and authentic stage presence. With 4+ years of experience hosting events, talkshows, competitions, and television programs, I bring energy, professionalism, and a warm touch to every stage — whether in Vietnamese, English, or Chinese.

I believe that every event is a unique story waiting to be told, and my role is to make that story unforgettable.
